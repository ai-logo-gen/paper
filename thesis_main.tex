\abstract{\textit{Can foundation models master simplicity?} Large-scale text-to-image models often struggle to produce the clean, structural aesthetics required for professional minimalist branding. This paper evaluates the resource-efficient multimodal specialization of a foundation model for minimalist logo generation. By fine-tuning Stable Diffusion v1.5 through a combination of Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) and ControlNet-based sketch conditioning, we address the paradox of adapting complex general-purpose models to highly constrained, simpler domains. Our systematic analysis of LoRA hyperparameters identifies optimal configurations that balance stylistic coherence with structural fidelity using only consumer-grade hardware. Evaluated via CLIP, FID, and SSIM metrics, we demonstrate that a compact dataset of 1,500 logos is sufficient to achieve high-quality, controllable design automation. This work validates that multimodal conditioning and efficient fine-tuning strategies effectively democratize access to professional-grade design tools.}
